COMMON TRADE TERMS

Terms are agreed per order. These are the ones our buyers use most often.

TERMWHAT IT MEANS FOR YOU
FOBWe deliver the goods on board your vessel at the Indian port. You arrange and pay for main freight and insurance.
CIFWe arrange and pay for freight and insurance to your destination port. You handle import clearance on arrival.
CFRAs CIF, but without insurance — useful if you already insure your cargo.
OtherAsk us about other Incoterms if your logistics need a different arrangement.
