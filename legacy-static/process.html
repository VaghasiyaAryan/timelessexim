i.

Enquiry & brief

YOU SHARE · WE CLARIFY

Tell us the products, specifications, quantities and target market you're sourcing for. A reference image, tech pack or target price is all we need to start.

  • Product, fabric and size requirements
  • Estimated quantities and delivery window
  • Destination country and port
ii.

Sampling

APPROVAL BEFORE BULK

We propose fabric and construction options, develop samples and courier them to you so you can approve fabric, colour, size and finish.

  • Quotation for your volumes and trade terms
  • Physical samples for sign-off
  • Revisions until the sample is right
iii.

Production & QC

INSPECTED AGAINST YOUR SAMPLE

Bulk production runs with in-line and final inspections. Every batch is checked against the approved sample before it moves to packing.

  • Weave, finish and colour-fastness checks
  • Measurement and stitching inspection
  • Milestone updates during production
iv.

Packing & labelling

READY FOR YOUR SHELVES

Private labels, barcodes, inserts and cartons prepared to your market's requirements, so goods arrive ready for retail or hospitality use.

  • Woven or printed labels, care labels
  • Polybags, retail boxes or bulk cartons
  • Carton marking to your specification
v.

Documentation & shipping

THROUGH TO YOUR PORT

Export paperwork, freight booking and dispatch coordinated through to your destination, under the Incoterms agreed for your order.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of origin and shipping documents
  • FOB, CIF and other Incoterms
QUALITY ASSURANCE

Every batch, checked twice.

Quality isn't a final step — it's built into production. We inspect during manufacturing and again before packing, always against the sample you approved.

  • In-line inspectionIssues caught during production, not after it.
  • Final inspectionWeave, finish, measurements and colour-fastness checked before packing.
  • Consistent lotsContainer-scale volumes with consistent lot quality on repeat orders.
