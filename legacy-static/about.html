02 WHAT WE DO

Everything between the mill and your port.

We handle the parts of international sourcing that usually take the most time — so you can focus on selling.

Sourcing

We match your brief to the right mills and manufacturing units across India's textile hubs.

Product development

Fabric, construction, size, colour and finish developed to your specification, with samples for approval.

Quality control

In-line and final inspections for weave, finish, measurements and colour-fastness against your approved sample.

Packing & labelling

Private labels, care labels, barcodes, inserts and retail-ready packaging prepared for your market.

Export documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and shipping documents for every order.

Shipping coordination

Freight booking and dispatch coordinated through to your destination port.
