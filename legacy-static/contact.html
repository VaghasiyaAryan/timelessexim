FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions from international buyers.

What is your minimum order quantity?
MOQs depend on the product, fabric and level of customisation. Standard ranges can start at smaller volumes, while custom developments and private-label programmes need higher quantities. Share your requirement and we'll confirm the MOQ with your quotation.
Can we receive samples before placing a bulk order?
Yes. We develop and courier samples so you can approve fabric, colour, size and finish before production. Sample and courier charges, and whether they can be adjusted against your bulk order, are confirmed at the enquiry stage.
Do you offer private labelling and custom packaging?
Yes. We can apply your woven or printed labels, care labels, barcodes and inserts, and pack into polybags, retail boxes or bulk cartons to your specification.
What are your production lead times?
Lead times vary with product, quantity, fabric availability and season. Once samples are approved we confirm a production and dispatch schedule, and keep you updated at each milestone.
Which countries do you export to?
We supply buyers across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Oceania — including the USA, Canada, the UK, Germany, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Australia. Other destinations are welcome; just tell us your port.
What trade terms and documents do you provide?
We work with common Incoterms such as FOB and CIF, agreed per order. Each shipment is supported by a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and the relevant shipping documents; payment terms are confirmed with your quotation.
